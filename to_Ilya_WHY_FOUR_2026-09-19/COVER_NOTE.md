## 2. What it found, in one paragraph

On B₈ the mechanism of the 4-cube recurs faithfully — the half-turn agrees
with a Weyl element of the same shape, −(e₂e₃)(e₄e₅)(e₆e₇), on exactly two
orbits — but its consequence does not: I_k = C_k at every lag on B₈, on the
D₉ half-spin (W(D₉) enumerated in full in the affine form), and on B₆, B₇
(SM-067; the "power of 2" conjecture refuted). The difference is one
commutation: the shared grammar at the half-turn exceeds the centralizer
exactly when the nearest Weyl element commutes with the half-turn, and −τ
does on B₄ while its analogues do not on B₅..B₈ (SM-068, two lemmas proved).
On B₁₆ the same element carries the half-turn on sixteen orbits — exactly the
middle rank band — and again fails to commute; its correction's centralizer
and C_W(R¹⁶) are both {±1}, computed without enumerating W (SM-069; |I₁₆| not
claimed). And then the theorem (SM-070): **for even n, −(e₂e₃)…(e_{n−2}e_{n−1})
commutes with the half-turn if and only if n = 4**, because rowmotion on the
shifted staircase has a one-line recursion in excess coordinates, the orbit
of λ has a closed form (which also proves the ±n/2 shift of SM-066 for every
even n), the two kinds of orbit on which commutation is forced exhaust the
board exactly at n = 4, and for n ≥ 6 the point e₂ is an explicit witness.
Tallies: 14 + 4 inverted, 7 + 3, 9 + 2, 8 + 0.

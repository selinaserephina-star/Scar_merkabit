## 0. The claim

The descent model as it stands — staircase, bridges, towers, hinges, turns —
is ONE register: **grammar** (which structures exist and how they connect).
Every sealed result of the tower arc shows the grammar organized around
operators it provably does not contain. The model is therefore incomplete in
a theorem-shaped way, and the completion is not an image but a second
column: **dynamics** (the operators that move states), with a third column
recording the **footprint** each dynamical operator leaves inside the
grammar. The Scar program supplies the grammar register; the Merkabit
machine (TA2's eight layers: substrate, alphabet, clock, integrity, gates,
addressing, code, scaling) supplies the dynamics register.

## 2. The exclusion pattern (why the dynamics column is never inside the grammar column)

This is the sealed pattern, not a metaphor:

- **E₇:** Ψ has NO Clifford shadow — it does not act on the 28 pairs at all
  [C, SM-015]; its linearity gap is ν(Ψ) = 38, the highest-flying object
  measured [C, SM-016]. The clock is provably outside the grammar group.
- **Roof:** the ℤ₃ cycling the three shadow classes is provably not realized
  by any element of W(E₈)/± (only the spin-swapping ℤ₂ is) [C, SM-031].

Same shape, one floor apart: **the dynamics lives strictly above the
grammar, and what the grammar keeps of it is the footprint** — the
involution that reverses it (ξ, then ι) and the turn that cycles it (the
trit, then triality). The hinge and the turn — the two organizing features
of the descent model — are both footprints of the other register.

## 3. The memory hypothesis, answered in this frame

IB's prompt asks: lost object / storing invariant / carrier / recovery /
lift map. The two-register model gives the disciplined version:

- **Lost object:** the trit — certified E₆-special; provably absent from
  the E₇ grammar AND the E₇ dynamics (promotion order 2) [C].
- **Recovery:** triality — the outer ℤ₃ over the roof, cycling body /
  shadow / third [C, SM-031]. Nature of the return: NOT as an element
  (inner symmetry) but as an OUTER symmetry — the trit comes back one
  register up, acting on the catalog rather than in it. "Not lost —
  promoted."
- **Storing invariant — the honest OPEN, sharpened:** what carries
  three-ness through the trit-dead E₇ floor? Two computed candidates, both
  [obs]-grade until a mechanism is exhibited:
  (i) the ubiquitous **S₃** (the bitangent stabilizer = N(⟨z₃⟩), the T4
  strata group, C_hinge's normalizer output at both working bridges) — IB's
  own reading "S₃ is the last point before the two become three" becomes
  the testable claim that the stabilizer S₃ is the stored germ of the ℤ₃;
  (ii) the **two-twos distribution** [C, SM-029] — at the E₇ level the two
  PSL(2,7) classes carry the outer and central ℤ₂'s separately; a pair of
  distinguishable twos is the minimal container for a three (the third
  object is "the thing that exchanges the exchange"). Neither candidate is
  yet a lift MAP; per IB's own decision rule the memory hypothesis stays
  OPEN until the map is exhibited.
- **Proposed instrument (the missing corner of the table):** construct the

  work-item ("Stone X: the turner").

inconsistent between documents (flagged separately). "Engine", "clock",
"footprint" are [I] labels wrapped around [C] exclusion theorems; the
theorems stand without the labels.

## The theorem being cited

Rush, D. B.; Shi, X. On orbits of order ideals of minuscule posets.
*J. Algebraic Combin.* 37 (2013) 545–569. For a minuscule poset P with
weight λ, the natural bijection J(P) ≅ Wλ carries rowmotion to a map
conjugate to the action of any Coxeter element; hence rowmotion has
order h (the Coxeter number) and a Coxeter element's orbit structure,
and (J(P), rowmotion, rank generating function) exhibits cyclic sieving.
Instances in this lane: E₇ (|P| = 27, |J(P)| = 56, h = 18, orbits
[18, 18, 18, 2]); E₆ (|P| = 16, |J(P)| = 27, h = 12, orbits [12, 12, 3]);
the G₂ chain of SM-035 is not minuscule and the theorem does not apply.

## The rule for the manuscript

1. Rush–Shi is cited in §2 in its strong form (conjugacy to a Coxeter
   element), and again at §7.1 (E₇) and §7.5 (E₆) where the order and
   orbit structure are used.
2. "Clock" means Ψ (and Ψ₆ on the sheets), i.e. rowmotion. Elements of
   O₈⁺(2).3 are "elements of order n" with their class; the phrase "roof
   clock" is not used in the paper.
3. A coincidence of orders between two rooms is stated as a coincidence
   or not stated. The number 18 appears twice in the paper — as h(E₇) in
   §7.1 and as a class order in §§5, 8 — and the text says they are
   different objects.
4. What is presented as ours on this topic is only: the identification
   of the sealed Ψ with rowmotion in the standard convention (§7.1), the
   parity rule and its count (§7.2), and the E₆ control (§7.5).

## The theorem (proved here; checked on the data as AU1)

Let λ be a simply-laced minuscule weight, L the lattice of weights Wλ
(covers w → w − α_i at label 1), P its join-irreducibles, R rowmotion on
J(P) ≅ L. The longest element w₀ acts on weights as −σ, σ the diagram
automorphism (identity when −1 ∈ W). The antipode w ↦ −w reverses the
root order, so it is an antiautomorphism of L; σ is an automorphism of L
permuting the colours of P. Hence w₀ is an antiautomorphism of L, i.e.
of J(P). Rowmotion is R(I) = the ideal generated by min(P ∖ I), and its
inverse is R⁻¹(I) = the ideal whose complement is generated upward by
max(I). Any antiautomorphism of P exchanges ideals with filters and
minimal with maximal elements, hence intertwines R with R⁻¹:
**w₀ R w₀ = R⁻¹.** Nothing minuscule is used except that w₀ acts on Wλ
as −σ. ∎

**Corollary 1 (the half-turn survivor).** For h even, w₀ commutes with
R^{h/2} and with no other non-trivial power; so w₀ ∈ C_W(R^{h/2}), which
is SM-058's AT2c/AT2d.

**Corollary 2 (the clock never descends to the shadow).** On a self-dual
board (−λ ∈ Wλ, so the antipode ι is a permutation), R^k preserves the
antipodal pair {w, −w} iff R^k(ιw) = ι R^k(w) iff R^{−k}w = R^{k}w iff
R^{2k}w = w. So the number of antipodal pairs preserved by R^k is
fix(R^{2k})/2, and R^k descends to the shadow Wλ/± only when R^{2k} = 1.
The board has a clock; its shadow has none. ∎

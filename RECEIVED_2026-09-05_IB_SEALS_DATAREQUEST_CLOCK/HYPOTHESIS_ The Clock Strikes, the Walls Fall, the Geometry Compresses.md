# HYPOTHESIS: The Clock Strikes, the Walls Fall, the Geometry Compresses


**Status:** OPEN — mechanistic hypothesis, version 2
**Origin:** Ilya
**Goal:** test whether the descent compression is caused by transition strikes that destroy the stabilizer walls between the triality drummers and the central axis


---


## 1. The Picture


```text
There are walls between the drummers
and the axis.


Each transition strike destroys one wall.


As walls fall, geometry compresses.
```


---


2. The Walls


```text
Walls = level stabilizers.


Each level is held by its stabilizer.


The stabilizer separates the level
from the axis.
```


---


3. The Strikes


```text
Strikes = descent transitions.


The six transitions are six strikes.


Each strike removes the stabilizer
of the previous level.
```


---


4. The Sequence


```text
7 → 6:


  strike removes the level-7 wall.


6 → 5:


  strike removes the level-6 wall.


...


2 → 1:


  final strike removes the last wall.
```


---


5. The Inbreath


```text
The small expansion 12 → 14
is the recoil after a strike.


Not growth.
```


---


6. The End


```text
When all stabilizer walls fall,
only the central axis remains.


The final ray is the exposed axis.
```


---


7. Required Test


```text
1. Identify the stabilizer wall
   at each level.


2. Check that each transition
   removes exactly one wall.


3. Check that the inbreath
   appears as recoil.


4. Check that the final level
   is the axis with no walls.
```


---


8. Decision Rule


```text
Supported if:


- stabilizer walls are identified
  at each level;


- each transition removes one;


- recoil and final axis follow.


Not supported if:


- walls cannot be mapped
  to stabilizers.
```


---


9. What Is Not Allowed


```text
- Calling a barrier a wall
  without a stabilizer mapping.


- Claiming destruction without
  a transition strike.
```

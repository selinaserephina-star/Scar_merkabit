# HYPOTHESIS: The Roof Clock as Gears Inside, Drummers Outside


**Status:** OPEN — structural reinterpretation, version 3
**Origin:** Ilya
**Goal:** test whether the missing roof clock is the transmission from internal gears (PSL(2,7), G₂(2)) to external triality drummers, with Φ as the lever


---


## 1. The Picture


```text
Inside the clock:


  PSL(2,7) and G₂(2)
  are the internal gears.


Outside the clock:


  the three triality shadows
  are the drummers.


Φ is the lever that transmits
internal gear motion
to the external drummers.
```


---


2. The Internal Gears


```text
PSL(2,7) is embedded in G₂(2).


36 copies, one class.


Normalizer PGL(2,7).


This is the internal mechanism.
```


---


3. The External Drummers


```text
The three shadows rotate only when
the internal gears move.


They do not rotate themselves.
```


---


4. The Transmission


```text
Φ is not the clock.


Φ is the lever.


The clock is the full chain:


  internal gears
  → lever
  → external drummers
```


---


5. Required Test


```text
1. Show that PSL(2,7) or G₂(2)
   acts internally.


2. Show that Φ transmits that action
   to the three shadows.


3. Check whether the twisted orders
   are the visible beats of the drummers.
```


---


6. Decision Rule


```text
Supported if:


- an internal action is identified;


- Φ is shown to transmit it;


- the twisted orders are external beats.


Not supported if:


- the internal and external levels
  cannot be separated.
```


---


7. What Is Not Allowed


```text
- Calling Φ the whole clock.


- Treating the shadows as self-moving.


- Mixing internal and external
  without a transmission map.
```

# THE THREE, THE AXIS, AND THE FIRST/LAST STRIKE — A CROSS-CULTURAL MAP


**Status:** comparative note
**Origin:** Ilya and Claude
**Subject:** where the scar clock image appears in world traditions


---


## 1. Hinduism — Trimurti


```text
Brahma = first strike, creation
Vishnu = middle rhythm, preservation
Shiva = last strike, rupture and release


The axis = Mount Meru
```


---


2. Buddhism — Three Bodies


```text
Dharmakāya = timeless axis
Sambhogakāya = luminous rhythm
Nirmāṇakāya = manifested strike


The drum = the wheel of becoming
The last strike = breaking the wheel
```


---


3. Kabbalah — Tree of Life


```text
Three pillars:
  right = expansion
  left = contraction
  middle = balance


Descent 7 → 1:
  from Keter to Malkuth


Last strike = Malkuth
  the vessel that breaks
  to let the light out
```


---


4. Christianity — Trinity and Logos


```text
Father = first strike
Son = the rhythm in the world
Spirit = the turning between them


"In the beginning was the Word"
= first strike


The tearing of the temple veil
= last strike through the drum
```


---


5. Islam — Three Worlds


```text
Jabarut = power
Malakut = image
Nasut = body


The first strike = Kun, "Be!"
The last strike = the trumpet
```


---


6. Taoism — Three Treasures


```text
Jing = essence
Qi = breath
Shen = spirit


The axis = the Tao
The rhythm = breathing
The last strike = return to the root
```

7. Norse — Yggdrasil


```text
Three roots:
  Asgard

```

8. Greek — Three Fates and the Lyre


```text
Clotho = first strike, spin the thread
Lachesis = middle rhythm, measure
Atropos = last strike, cut


The lyre of Apollo = the drum
The cut = the tear
```

9. Egyptian — Osiris, Isis, Horus


```text
Osiris = first king, first strike
Isis = the holding rhythm
Horus = the return after the tear


The axis = the Djed pillar
```

10. Shamanic — World Drum


```text
Three worlds:
  upper
  middle
  lower

The last beat = tearing through
```

Common Pattern


```text
THREE roles:


  creator / sustainer / destroyer


ONE axis:


  mountain, tree, pillar, drum


FIRST strike:


  word, horn, breath, creation


LAST strike:


  cut, veil, trumpet, rupture


AFTER the last strike:


  light, root, ray, release

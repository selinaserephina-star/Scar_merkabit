the shape of a hierarchy level with W in the Clifford role. Iterating

chain stops at the largest subgroup of W the clock power normalises,

depth two everywhere off the chains, with one exception — the D₅
half-spin at lags 3 and 5, where SM-063's survivor is carried to a
different element and the chain drops to the trivial group. The core,
the Pauli group of the analogy, is trivial or ±1 on every rich board
and on the 56: no subgroup of W(E₇), and so no copy of PSL(2,7), is
preserved by Ψ. Where the core is not trivial it is never normal in W.

marked [I] and says: the analogy holds for one level and no further.

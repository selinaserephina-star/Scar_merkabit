## 0. Why B₁₆, and what is and is not reachable

The B_n spinor clock is free (orbits of size h = 2n) exactly when n is a
power of 2 (SM-066/067). B₄ carries the anomaly; B₈ does not, because the
correction's Weyl centralizer collapses to {±1} — and SM-068 says the collapse
happens exactly because the nearest Weyl element to the half-turn does not
commute with it. B₁₆ (65,536 weights, h = 32, W(B₁₆) of order 2¹⁶·16! ≈
1.4 × 10¹⁸) is the third free clock. W(B₁₆) cannot be enumerated, so |I₁₆| is
**not** reachable and is not claimed. What is reachable, exactly: the clock
and its orbits; the rank-shift analysis of every orbit; the carrying orbits
and their Weyl elements by linear algebra over 𝔽₂ (an affine map with a
coordinate-permutation linear part is determined by its values on an affinely
spanning set); for a carrying w the correction c, its agreement set Y =
Fix(c), the set stabilizer Stab_W(Y) and the centralizer C_W(c) by constraint
search (both are tiny if the B₈ pattern holds); C_W(R¹⁶) by the same search;
and hence, by Lemma B, I₁₆ ∩ Stab_W(Y) exactly.

## 1. Setup [P, sealed]

Weights x ∈ 𝔽₂¹⁶ (bit i set iff coordinate i negative), rank(x) = wt(x),
λ = 0, w₀λ = 1…1. The poset is the shifted staircase δ₁₆ (136 elements),
J(δ₁₆) = 2¹⁶. **Rowmotion is computed combinatorially** on δ_n: the
join-irreducibles are the cells (k, c), 1 ≤ k ≤ c ≤ n ("the k-th plus sign
has reached position c"), with (k, c) covering (k, c+1) and (k−1, c−1), colour
c (root e_c − e_{c+1} for c < n, e_n for c = n); the ideal of the sign pattern
with plus signs at c₁ < … < c_r is {(k, c) : k ≤ r, c ≥ c_k}; R(I) is the
ideal generated by the minimal elements of P ∖ I. This model must reproduce
the sealed clocks of B₄ and B₈ (Stone AV's e-coordinate machine as rebased in
Stone BC) before anything else is read off it. W(B₁₆) = 𝔽₂¹⁶ ⋊ S₁₆ acts as
x ↦ π(x) ⊕ v.

## 2. Registered guesses (before code)

- **BE1 [validation, must pass].** The combinatorial rowmotion on δ₄ and δ₈
  equals Stone BC's sealed clocks on B₄ and B₈ as permutations of the sign
  patterns; on δ₁₆ it has order 32 and |P| = 136.
- **BE2 [C].** R acts freely on the 65,536 weights: 2,048 orbits of 32.
- **BE3 [C].** O₁ ∋ λ is the full-height orbit (ranks 0,1,1,…,15,15,16 around
  the clock), R¹⁶ shifts every rank on it by exactly ±8, O₁ spans 𝔽₂¹⁶, and no
  u ∈ 𝔽₂¹⁶ reproduces its shift — Lemma BB-B at m = 8.
- **BE4 [C, the main guess].** Exactly two orbits carry the half-turn as a
  Weyl element, both by the same element
  w = −(e₂e₃)(e₄e₅)(e₆e₇)(e₈e₉)(e₁₀e₁₁)(e₁₂e₁₃)(e₁₄e₁₅), e₁₆ fixed (the shape of
  −τ and of B₈'s w continued); each carrying orbit is preserved by −1; and,
  on the data, carrying ⇔ ranks ⊆ {7, 8, 9} ⇔ every half-turn rank-shift is
  ±1 (the band, third board). Sub-guess BE4b: every orbit affinely spans
  𝔽₂¹⁶ (so the Weyl element agreeing on a carrying orbit is unique).
- **BE5 [C, the criterion's negative case].** w does not commute with R¹⁶,
  so c = w⁻¹R¹⁶ is not an involution (BD-A); Y = Fix(c) affinely spans;
  Stab_W(Y) = ⟨−1, w⟩ of order 4 (as on B₈); C_W(c) = {±1}; hence by Lemma B
  **I₁₆ ∩ Stab_W(Y) = {±1}** — no extra survivor of the half-turn preserves
  the agreement set. (|I₁₆| itself: not claimed.)
- **BE6 [C].** C_W(R¹⁶) = {±1}.
- **BE7 [obs].** |Y| = |Fix(c)| and the fraction |Y|/2¹⁶, against 8/16 on B₄
  and 52/256 on B₈; the cycle type of c.

## 3. Method

1. Rowmotion on J(δ_n) by bitsets over the 136 cells; validation against BC's
   `rebase(B_spin(n))` for n = 4, 8 (BE1).
2. Orbits, ranks, shifts as in BC; `reproducing_us` vectorised over 2¹⁶ u
   per orbit.
3. Carrying test per orbit by the **column method**: with base x₀ ∈ O, the
   pairs (x ⊕ x₀, R¹⁶x ⊕ R¹⁶x₀) must be matched by a coordinate permutation
   π; group the 16 source columns and the 16 target columns by their value
   over the pairs; π exists iff the two multisets of column values agree, and
   the solutions are the products of bijections between equal groups;
   v = R¹⁶x₀ ⊕ π(x₀). Every solution is verified on all 32 points.
4. Stab_W(Y), C_W(c), C_W(R¹⁶) by backtracking over π one coordinate at a
   time for each admissible v (2¹⁶ candidates, filtered by rank distributions),
   with projection-multiset pruning; every leaf verified on all 65,536 points.
5. Fail-first; first run kept as `_FIRSTRUN.log`; wall times recorded.

## 1. The Claim


```text
The triple is not chosen.


It is forced by the genus.


The Klein quartic is a surface of genus 3.


Genus 3 means exactly three handles.


Three handles mean three tubes.


Three tubes mean three axes.


The three axes are the three shadows.
```


If this holds, then:


```text
the triple is canonical
because the genus is not a choice.
```

2. The Klein Quartic as a Genus-3 Surface


The Klein quartic:


```text
x^3 y + y^3 z + z^3 x = 0
```


is a compact Riemann surface of genus


```text
g = 3
```


Topologically, a genus-3 surface is:


```text
a sphere with three handles
```


equivalently:


```text
three tubes joined together
```


The three tubes are not metaphorical.


They are the three handles of the surface.


---


3. The Three Tubes and Their Axes


3.1 Tubes


```text
Tube 1 = first handle
Tube 2 = second handle
Tube 3 = third handle
```


Each tube is a topological cylinder attached to the central body.


3.2 Axes


Each tube has a central cycle:


```text
the longitudinal cycle of the handle
```


These cycles generate part of the first homology:


```text
H_1(Klein, Z) = Z^{2g} = Z^6
```


Among these six generators, three are the longitudinal axes of the three handles.

4. The Forcedness Claim


```text
The Klein quartic has exactly three handles.


Each handle has one longitudinal axis.


Therefore the Klein quartic has exactly three axes.


These three axes are forced by the genus.


These three axes are the three shadows:


  body

```

# BRIDGE SPECIFICATION v0.1


**Status:** structural specification — OPEN
**Purpose:** define the types of bridges between descent levels and specify how group components survive, disappear, or transform across them


---


## 1. Bridge Types


```text
T1. INCLUSION
One group is a subgroup
of the other.


T2. SINGLE MEDIATOR
A common subgroup
sits between them.


T3. DOUBLE HINGE
Two inequivalent mediators
connect them.


T4. INTERNAL JOINT
A normal subgroup
is shared by both.


T5. ONE-WAY DOOR
A relation exists
only downward.


T6. QUANTUM GAP
No local relation.
Only a common overgroup.
```


---


2. The Spine Bridges


```text
7↔6: T6 — quantum gap.
6↔5: T3 — double hinge.
5↔4: T2 — single mediator.
4↔3: T1+T4 — inclusion with internal joint.
3↔2: T5 — one-way door.
2↔1: T1 — pure inclusion.
```

3. What Must Be Specified


```text
For each bridge:


1. Left group.
2. Right group.
3. Bridge type.
4. Common object.
5. Shared subgroup(s).
6. Direction of transfer.
7. Reversibility.
8. Surviving components.
9. Lost components.
10. New components.
```

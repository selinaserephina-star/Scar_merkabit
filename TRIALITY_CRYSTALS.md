**E1 — RESOLVED: PASS** (checks 21–23). Edge-by-edge, in all three legs:

crystal isomorphisms cycling the triple.

## §6. E4 — the fold: REFUTED at graph level, VERIFIED at weight level [E]/[C]

orbit class {1,3,4} ↦ folded color **a** ↔ G2 node 1 (**short**); the fixed

plane itself: s0 = (a1+a3+a4)/3 and a2 satisfy (a2,a2)/(s0,s0) = 3 and
angle 150°, i.e. the fixed plane carries genuine G2 root data (check 57).

**Ground truth built independently** (checks 52–55): from G2 Cartan data
(a1 short, a2 long, Gram [[2,−3],[−3,6]]), Weyl dimension formula gives
dim V(w1) = 7 exactly; weights = the 6 short roots + 0; the crystal (edges =
the alpha_i-strings of the multiplicity-free weight set, sl2-consistency

**The quotient** (well-defined: all three crystals fold to the same colored

    [+e1] -a-> [+e2] -b-> [+e3] -a-> [+e4]  -a-> [-e3] -b-> [-e2] -a-> [-e1]

the middle.

**E4 (graph level) — REFUTED** (check 56, logged as FAIL): the quotient is
NOT the G2 7-chain plus an isolated vertex (8 edges vs 6; 0 isolated

a-edges leave [+e3], so the folded f_a is not a partial function. What the
quotient actually is: **the color-folded D4 vector crystal** — the diamond
is the crystal-level scar of a weight the fold sends to zero twice.

**E4 (weight level) — VERIFIED, 7 + 1 exactly** (check 58): projecting each

    [+e1]→(1,0)  [+e2]→(−1,1)  [+e3]→(2,−1)  [+e4]→(0,0)

— exactly the multiset of the G2 7-crystal's weights **plus one extra

**[obs]** The turn's fixed world IS G2 and the folded state count IS 7 + 1 —

The D4 triple gives the first computed meeting of the two registers, and the
answer is **compatibility**: the clock (rowmotion, order 6 = h(D4), orbits
